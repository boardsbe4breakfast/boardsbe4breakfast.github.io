Privacy Policy: BoardsBe4Breakfast Social Media Integrations

Last updated: 2026-10-31

This policy covers the social media integrations BoardsBe4Breakfast (the shop) is setting up to publish to and manage the shop's own accounts on social platforms — currently Instagram and Pinterest. Today the shop posts and pins by hand; an integration only becomes active after the platform approves it and the shop connects it to its own account. This page describes what an active integration would store, where, why, and how to ask questions or stop it. It does not cover the platforms themselves (Instagram, Pinterest, and any other platform have their own privacy policies that govern your account there), and it does not cover Etsy, eBay, PayPal, or any other service the shop uses to sell.

1. What we collect

When the shop connects an integration to a social platform, the platform sends us an access token (and, where the platform uses them, a refresh token). These are secret credentials that let the integration act on the shop's own account, within the permissions approved at connection time.

Each integration is limited to the shop's own accounts:

  • Instagram: publish posts and read the shop's own post metrics.
  • Pinterest: create and manage pins and boards; no Pinterest data is read.

The integrations do not collect personal data about anyone else: no names, email addresses, passwords, contact lists, follower lists, messages, or profile data of other users. We do not ask anyone for their platform login details. If an integration ever starts handling more than this, we will update this section first.

2. Why we need it

We use the tokens for one purpose: to publish and manage content on the shop's own social media accounts, which link to the shop's product listings, and to see how that content performs.

3. Where it is stored

The tokens are stored in a private configuration file on a private server that the shop owner controls. The file is not published, not part of any public code repository, and not shared with anyone. Only the shop owner's own automation tools read the file.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent, or share the tokens or any data obtained through a platform's API with any third party, except service providers (such as our hosting and AI automation tooling) that process it solely on our instructions to run the integrations. The only party that receives a token itself is the platform that issued it, when we request, refresh, or use it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep each integration's tokens until its access is revoked or the shop decommissions the integration; decommissioning includes deleting the stored tokens. Post metrics are read on demand to guide the shop's own marketing, and summary figures may be kept in the shop's internal business records.

6. Your choices and data deletion

  • Access can be revoked at any time from the connected account's app settings on the platform. Revoking invalidates the tokens immediately.
  • To request deletion of any stored token or data, email us (see section 8) and we will delete it and confirm promptly.
  • Because these integrations only act on the shop's own accounts, we hold no personal data about other platform users to delete; if you believe we hold data about you, contact us and we will check and respond.

7. Children

The integrations are for adult business use. They are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
