Privacy Policy: BoardsBe4Breakfast Pinterest Integration

Last updated: 2026-10-31

This policy covers the Pinterest integration run by BoardsBe4Breakfast (the shop). It describes what we store, where, why, and how to ask questions or stop the integration. It applies only to the Pinterest integration. It does not cover Etsy, eBay, PayPal, or any other service the shop uses, and it does not cover Pinterest itself, whose privacy policy governs your Pinterest account.

1. What we collect

When you approve the shop's Pinterest app, Pinterest sends us an access token and a refresh token. These are secret credentials that let the shop act on the Pinterest account it is connected to, within the permissions you approved.

The integration is set up to create pins and boards only. It does not read any Pinterest data yet; if that changes, this section will be updated. It does not keep a copy of your name, email address, password, contact list, followers, or any other Pinterest profile data. We do not ask for your Pinterest login details.

2. Why we need it

We use the tokens for one purpose: to create and manage pins and boards on the shop's own Pinterest account, which links to the shop's Etsy listings.

3. Where it is stored

The tokens are stored in a private configuration file on a private server that the shop owner controls. The file is not published, not part of any public code repository, and not shared with anyone. Only the shop owner's own automation tools read the file.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent, or share the tokens or any data obtained through the Pinterest API with any third party. The only party that receives the tokens is Pinterest, when we request, refresh, or use them.

5. How long we keep it

We keep the tokens until you revoke the app's access or the shop stops the integration, whichever comes first. When the integration stops, the shop deletes the stored tokens.

6. Your choices

  • You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Pinterest account settings. If you can't find the setting, contact us and we'll remove the app for you.
  • Revoking stops the shop from using the connection straight away.
  • You can ask us to delete any stored token by contacting us (see section 8).

7. Children

The integration is for adult business use. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
